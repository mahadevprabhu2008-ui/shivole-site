Last updated 25 July 2026 · Operated by Mahadevprabhu N, India

Shivole acts inside your other accounts, so this policy is specific about what it touches. If anything here is unclear, ask us at mahadevprabhu2008@gmail.com.

We never receive your password. Sign-in happens on Google's, Apple's or GitHub's own page. We only get a signed confirmation that it was you.

Connected apps

When you connect an app such as Gmail or GitHub, that provider gives us an access key scoped to what you agreed to on their consent screen. We store it encrypted, tied to your account alone, and use it only to carry out things you ask for.

Disconnecting deletes the key from our database. You can also revoke access from the provider's own security settings at any time.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of services to run Shivole. Each sees only what it needs:

  • Google (Gemini API) — receives the text of your request to generate a reply
  • Neon — our database host, where accounts and conversations are stored
  • Expo — delivers push notifications, if enabled
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play — handle subscriptions. We never see your card details
  • OpenAI — receives voice recordings for transcription, if you use voice
While Shivole is in development we use Google's free API tier, under which Google may review content to improve their services. We will move to a paid tier, where that does not apply, before opening to the public. This notice will be updated when we do.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, or share it for advertising
  • We don't read your connected accounts except to carry out a request
  • We don't send, post, book or buy anything without you approving it on screen

Your choices

  • See what's remembered — the Memory tab lists every fact, and lets you delete any of them
  • Clear your history — delete conversations at any time; the rest expires after 90 days
  • Disconnect an app — removes its key immediately
  • Delete your account — in Account settings. This removes your profile, conversations, remembered facts and every connected app key. It cannot be undone

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, correct it, or object to how it's handled. Write to mahadevprabhu2008@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Connected app keys are encrypted before they're stored. Access to your data requires a token that expires and is rotated. Traffic to our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfect, and we'll tell you promptly if something happens that affects you.

Children

Shivole isn't for people under 16. We don't knowingly collect their data, and will delete it if we learn we have.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
