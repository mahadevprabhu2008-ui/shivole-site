What it actually does

You ask in your own words. It figures out the steps, then brings you the finished thing to check.

Writes your mail

Finds the right contact, drafts in your voice, lands it in your Drafts folder. You read it before anyone else does.

Handles your code inbox

Pulls the issues and pull requests waiting on you, and drafts the reply you were putting off.

Compares before you buy

What it won't do

These aren't features we haven't got to. They're decisions.

neverSends, posts, books or buys without you tapping approve on screen first.
neverSees your password. You sign in on Google's or GitHub's own page, not ours.
neverKeeps a connected app's access after you disconnect it. The key is deleted, not disabled.
neverHolds your chats forever. They're deleted after 90 days, and you can clear them sooner.
neverSells your data. There's no version of this where that pays better than the subscription.
